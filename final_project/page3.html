// Exploring Gear

Urban explorers can use different types of gear depending on the location they are visiting. Cameras are commonly used to document buildings, while flashlights can help when exploring dark areas. Comfortable clothing and sturdy shoes are also useful when walking through older buildings. Other equipment, such as gloves, masks, and backpacks, can be used to carry equipment and provide some protection while exploring.
